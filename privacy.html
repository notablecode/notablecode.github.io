Privacy Policy

Notable Code builds software and publishes a few public projects. This policy explains what data those projects touch, which is very little, and what we do with it.

Effective September 2, 2026.

Who this covers

This policy applies to everything Notable Code operates:

  • notablecode.com, this website.
  • standings.notablecode.com, the College Football Bully Index standings site.
  • The Bully Index YouTube channel (@bullyindex) and the videos published there.
  • The Bully Index uploader, a Google application Notable Code uses to publish videos to that channel through the YouTube API.

Our websites

Our websites are static pages. They have no accounts, no sign-in, no comment forms, and no advertising. We do not set cookies, and we do not run analytics or tracking scripts.

A few things happen on any website that are worth stating plainly:

  • Hosting logs. notablecode.com is served by GitHub Pages and standings.notablecode.com by Netlify. Like every web host, they may record standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and operational purposes under their own privacy policies. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.
  • Fonts and images. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the standings site loads team logos from ESPN's content network. Your browser requests those files directly, so those providers receive your IP address as part of serving them.
  • Contact. If you email or call us, we keep that correspondence to reply to you and for our own records. We do not add you to mailing lists.

The Bully Index YouTube channel

Videos are hosted by YouTube. When you watch, like, comment, or subscribe, YouTube and Google collect data according to the Google Privacy Policy. Notable Code sees only the aggregated statistics YouTube shows to channel owners, such as view counts. We do not export, sell, or combine that data with anything else.

The Bully Index uploader application

The Bully Index uploader is a small program that runs on Notable Code's own computer. It exists for one purpose: to upload each week's episode, its title and description, and its thumbnail to the Bully Index channel. It is used only by Notable Code with our own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's Google account is ever connected to it.

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it we agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles your data.

What it accesses

  • The single Google permission "Manage your YouTube videos" (the youtube.upload scope), which allows uploading videos and setting their thumbnails on the channel the account owner selects.
  • It does not read subscriber lists, comments, viewer data, analytics, email, contacts, or any other Google account data.

How it stores data

  • The authorization token Google issues is stored in a file on Notable Code's own computer and is used only to perform uploads. It is never transmitted anywhere except to Google's API, and it is never shared with third parties.
  • The application keeps no database of users and collects no personal information.

Revoking access

Access granted to the application can be removed at any time from the third-party access page of the Google account, after which the stored token stops working and is deleted.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named above, who process it only to host or deliver our sites and videos.

Children

Our sites and channel are general-audience sports content and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Substantive changes to how the uploader application handles Google data will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Notable Code using the contact details on our home page.
